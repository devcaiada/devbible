# Production-Readiness Checklist ✅🚀

A battle-tested 20-item checklist to verify security, reliability, scalability, UX, and compliance before shipping software to production.

> [!TIP]
> **Ready to audit your own app?** Copy our ready-to-use **[Project Review Checklist Template](PROJECT-REVIEW-CHECKLIST.md)** directly into your project repo or GitHub Issues!
>
> Not sure where to start? Check out our **[Production Maturity Levels & Badges](MATURITY-LEVELS.md)** to prioritize items based on whether you are launching an MVP (Level 1), scaling a SaaS (Level 2), or operating an enterprise platform (Level 3).

## 🎯 How to Use in GitHub Projects
You can turn this checklist into an interactive tracking board in your repository:
1. Open your repository's **Projects** tab.
2. Create a new Board or Table view called **Production Launch Checklist**.
3. Import the 20 items and assign owners to backend, frontend, and devops tasks.
